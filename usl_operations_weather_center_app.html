It pulls official USL schedule pages, matches home teams to venue coordinates, retrieves NOAA/NWS forecasts and active alerts, overlays SPC Day 1-3 categorical severe-weather outlooks, then renders a risk table, elevated-risk operations panel, and recommended league actions. ## What it pulls - Official schedule pages: - USL Championship: `https://www.uslchampionship.com/league-schedule` - USL League One: `https://www.uslleagueone.com/League-Schedule` - USL Super League / Gainbridge Super League: `https://www.gainbridgesuperleague.com/league-schedule/` - NWS forecast and alert data: - `https://api.weather.gov/points/{lat},{lon}` - `forecastHourly`, `forecast`, `forecastGridData` - `https://api.weather.gov/alerts/active?point={lat},{lon}` - SPC categorical outlook GeoJSON: - `https://www.spc.noaa.gov/products/outlook/day1otlk_cat.nolyr.geojson` - `https://www.spc.noaa.gov/products/outlook/day2otlk_cat.nolyr.geojson` - `https://www.spc.noaa.gov/products/outlook/day3otlk_cat.nolyr.geojson` ## Why the backend is required The browser should not directly scrape schedule sites or call every weather endpoint. It assigns points for: - active NWS warnings/watches/advisories, - thunderstorm/lightning wording in the NWS hourly forecast window, - precipitation probability and heavy-rain/flood wording, - SPC categorical outlook overlap at the venue point, - heat/applicable apparent-temperature signal, - wind/gust signal. Risk levels: - `Critical`: score >= 85 - `High`: score >= 60 - `Moderate`: score >= 30 - `Low`: score < 30 - `Unknown`: insufficient venue/weather data ## Radar `api.weather.gov` does not provide radar imagery for display.
